# CantyTech Website

## Design Context

Full detail in [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md) — read it before any design work.

- **Register:** brand (marketing/services site — design IS the product)
- **Platform:** web (Hugo static site)
- **Audience:** everyday people/households first, small businesses second; often older or less tech-confident visitors
- **Voice:** approachable, expert, trustworthy — plain English, no jargon, one person not a company
- **Anti-reference:** current placeholder styling (dark SaaS gradient hero, generic startup copy) is explicitly the wrong look
